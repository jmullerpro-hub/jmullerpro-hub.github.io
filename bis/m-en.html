Your data

Your data stays yours. No SnowLine account. Your tracks and health data stay on your iPhone. If you create a group, only your top speed, vertical and run count are shared, through iCloud.

Apple Health. Your ski days saved as workouts, without duplicating your Apple Watch.

Official bulletins. Avalanche risk comes only from official bulletins, relayed unchanged.
