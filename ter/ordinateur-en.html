SnowLine in action

A look at the app, from the dashboard to the 3D replay. Turn the sound on.

Every day gets its own summary

In the app, a ring for each day sits at the top of the screen. Tap one and it opens that day's summary: distance, vertical, time skiing down and falls, then your stats, your best run and the list of all your runs. A button opens the day's charts.

Relive every run on real terrain

A camera follows you down the mountain with your speed live. Put a 3D model of your run on the table and spin it with your fingertips.

Real terrain, a blue-to-red trail by speed. Chase, Side and Top views, plus Cinema mode.

Your speed, traced across the mountain

Blue to red, turn after turn, on a winter relief map, with the resort's runs and lifts and their names.

Start and finish altitudes of every run and lift. Offline maps: your resort stays readable without signal.

On the way down, and after

No need to take your phone out

Your live session on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

A fall? SnowLine notices

After an impact followed by no movement, SnowLine asks if you're OK.

Challenge your friends

Create a group, invite your friends and compare top speed, vertical and distance on a leaderboard.

Your best run, in a story

One tap on “Video” and SnowLine films your run: 20 seconds on real terrain, with your speed on the gauge.

Ski areas worldwide

Runs and lifts of thousands of ski areas, with their names.

Your data stays yours

On your iPhone

No SnowLine account. Your tracks and health data stay on your iPhone. If you create a group, only your top speed, vertical and run count are shared, through iCloud.

Apple Health

Your ski days saved as workouts, without duplicating your Apple Watch.

Official bulletins

Avalanche risk comes only from official bulletins, relayed unchanged.
